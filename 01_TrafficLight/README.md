# Traffic Light

Two synchronized traffic lights: one for cars (red / yellow / green) and one for pedestrians (red / green).
When one light is green, the other is red. Before switching, the green light blinks 3 times and the yellow light turns on.

**Components:** Arduino Uno, 5 LEDs (2 red, 2 green, 1 yellow), 5 × 220 Ω resistors, wires.
**Pins:** car light 13 / 12 / 11, pedestrian light 7 / 6.
**Concepts:** `digitalWrite`, `delay`, `for` loops.
